# BlastX Phantom sound pack

Test-copy integration. When using the Phantom with BlastX selected, blatxbullet.WAV supplies firing and the MP3 from each blastx_1_kill.zip through blastx_5_kills.zip supplies kills 1-5. After kill five, the sound sequence repeats from one, following the existing custom-sequence convention. A new kill interrupts the previous kill sound. ZIP preview PNGs are not installed as HUD graphics.

Files are copied byte-for-byte to app/mods/media/blastx-phantom/fire.wav and kill1.mp3 through kill5.mp3. They are decoded once through the shared local-audio preload; no file access or decoding occurs per shot/kill. Sounds route through the existing game's audio master and respect training mute, master volume and kill-sound enablement. Gain is 1 relative to those controls; original files, durations and pitch are unchanged.

Packaged sounds are scoped to Phantom + BlastX. Default Phantom and other weapons retain their existing behavior. Vandal custom sounds/kill sequences and Gaia-specific firing remain separate; Gaia still uses 0.55 gain. Saved local audio uploads are not replaced. The shared engine hooks are generated by build-audio-loader.cjs for both served asset layouts.

Hidden offline-audio test test-blastx-audio.cjs verifies six decoded files, firing routing, kill order and wrap, interruption, no per-shot fetch, default-Phantom/other-weapon isolation and mute/volume guards. Gaia audio regression and sniper native ADS timing checks also pass. Exact file comparison validates the original WAV and all five archived MP3 streams. Gameplay/manual listening is left to the user as requested.

Kill-sequence gain adjusted to 0.65 (35% lower gain). BlastX firing remains at 1.0 and Gaia firing at 0.55. Original audio bytes and sequence timing remain unchanged.
