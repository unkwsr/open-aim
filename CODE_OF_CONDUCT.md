# Code of conduct

open-aim is a place to get better at aiming and to build things together. Keep it friendly.

## Expected

- Be welcoming to new players and first-time contributors.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and explain why when you say no.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Posting someone else's private information.
- Spam, or advertising cheats, boosting or account selling.
- Sharing files you don't have the right to share.

## What happens

Maintainers can edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who
break these rules. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer listed on the
GitHub profile of the repository owner.
